# Switch: juice and satisfaction spec

Files in this folder:

- `juice.css`, `juice.js`: the effects layer. No dependencies.
- `themes.css` (generated by `gen_themes.py`): five full-world looks. Pure CSS under `:root[data-skin]`, so adding a look never touches game code.
- `demo.src.html` + `build.py` -> `switch-juice-demo.html`: playable lab and run that uses the layer. Same ids and class names as the live game, so it diffs cleanly.
- Live mockup: see the Artifact linked in the thread (separate from the game artifact, which the speed-game thread owns).

Written against the game version that has combo glow, pop-ups, rank titles, skins, sound packs and surprise events. Revision 2 (after feedback): milestone moments moved to slides between questions, richer themes, and a look and sound menu inside the game.

## Design principles

1. Every input gets an answer within 100 ms: sound, movement, colour.
1b. Anything big (streak milestones, double points) waits for the gap between questions and plays as its own slide, so it never competes with the puzzle you are solving.
2. Effects scale with the streak, so a long run feels different from the first answer.
3. The only boldness lives in the moments that matter (tier hits, level up, new best). Ordinary correct answers stay small so the big ones read.
4. Nothing blocks input. Particles, flashes and pops are pointer-events none and never delay the next question except the type-switch curtain (already a deliberate 0.5 s beat).
5. Everything follows the skin tokens (`--c`, `--ink`, `--panel`, `--good`, `--bad`) and the sound pack (waveform and pitch), so unlocks keep working.

## Looks (themes.css)

Each look is a whole scene, not a recolour: its own palette, display font, background scenery, card shape, button shape and particle shape.

| Look | Scenery | Card and controls | Particles | Fonts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Classic | Plain dark | Rounded cards | Dots and chips | Bricolage Grotesque, JetBrains Mono |
| Jungle | Canopy light, palm fronds in the corners, drifting fireflies | Leaf-shaped card corners, pill tags, a frond growing out of the card | Falling, swaying leaves | Lilita One |
| Cyberpunk | Neon skyline, scrolling perspective grid floor, scanlines | Square card with targeting-bracket corners, notched buttons, chromatic-split numbers, glow that grows with streak | Glitch bars | Orbitron, Share Tech Mono |
| Deep sea | Light rays swaying from above, rising bubbles | Very round cards, bubble pips | Bubbles that float up | Quicksand |
| Notebook (light) | Graph paper with a red margin line | Hand-drawn wobbly card, tape strip, hard paper shadow, marker-colour tags | Ink blots | Patrick Hand |

How a look plugs in: set `document.documentElement.dataset.skin = id`. Looks can also set `--jx-shape` (dot, leaf, glitch, bubble, ink), `--jx-p1`/`--jx-p2` (extra particle colours) and `--slide-bg`. Ambient animation stops under reduced motion. Keep the unlock levels the game already uses; the theme ids are classic, jungle, cyber, deep, notebook, replacing the older ember, ocean, neon and paper skins (save-file note: map ember to jungle, ocean to deep, neon to cyber, paper to notebook).

## Look and sound menu

- Start screen: a "Look and sound" card with a swatch preview per look, the sound packs, an Effects on/off toggle and a Sound toggle.
- In a run: a "Look & sound" link under the card opens the same panel as a bottom sheet. The timer is paused while it is open and answers are ignored. Changes apply instantly, and the chosen pack plays a short preview.
- Sound packs now: Classic, Chip, Bell, Arcade, plus two new voices, Marimba (wooden overtone) and Synth (low-passed saw).

## Timing table (ms)

Correct wash 380, thump 300, ring 550, pop 850, streak slide 1400 (in 320, out 230), wrong shake 400, wrong wash 450, curtain 660, event slide 1400, score count-up 260 in run and 1300 on the results screen, level-up auto close 3200.

## Sound design

- Master compressor so stacked sounds never clip. All sounds are synthesised (oscillators and one noise buffer), so no audio files.
- Scale: major pentatonic from C4 (261.63 Hz). Reason: any two notes sound fine together, so fast answering never produces a clash.
- The unlocked sound pack still applies: its waveform replaces the main voice and its `k` multiplies pitch. Pass `getPack()` to `Juice.init`.
- Audio starts only after the first click or key (`Juice.unlock()`), because browsers require it.

## Accessibility

- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no shake, thump, ring, curtain motion, callout motion, particles or confetti. Pops and callouts still appear, static. Sound, colour and haptics stay.
- `Juice.muted` follows the existing Sound toggle. `Juice.enabled = false` returns the flat game, which is handy for a "Reduce effects" setting.
- Nothing flashes more than once per answer, and the full-screen flash tops out at about 30% opacity. No effect strobes.
- Colour is never the only signal: wrong answers shake and show the `-2s` text, correct ones show `+points`.

   `var r=Juice.correct({stage:st,streak:g.streak,points:gained}); if(r.slide)g.pending.push(Juice.streakSlide(g.streak))`. The slide plays later, between questions (step 5).

   `Juice.wrong({stage:$('stage'),timeEl:$('time'),railEl:$('rail'),lostStreak:g.streak,penalty:PENALTY})` (call before `g.streak=0`).

5. `nextQ()`: after setting the question content and pausing, run the queued slides, then the type-switch curtain, then `begin` (see `runPending` and `nextQ` in `demo.src.html`):
   `runPending(function(){ hold=changed?Juice.switchTo({stage:st,name:T.n,glyph:Juice.glyph(g.type),ms:620}):0; setTimeout(begin,hold) })`. `runPending` shifts one item from `g.pending`, calls `Juice.slide({stage,...item,onDone})`, and repeats. You can delete `.switchin` markup and CSS.
6. `fireEvent()`: instead of showing a pop mid-question, push `{kind:'event',kicker:'Surprise',title:'Double points',sub:'for the next 10 seconds',chip:'2× score'}` onto `g.pending` and set `g.doubleUntil=g.clock+10` when the slide is shown (the clock is paused during the slide). 7. `tick()`: once per whole second call `Juice.lowTime(Math.ceil(left),{timeEl:$('time'),railEl:$('rail')})`. On finish and quit call `Juice.lowOff({railEl:$('rail')})`.
8. `finish()`: `Juice.end({newBest})`, then `Juice.countUp($('rScore'),0,g.score,1300,fillBars)`. Start the type bars at width 0 and set `data-w`. 9. Delete the old `.stage.ok`, `.stage.bad` keyframes and `.pop`/`popin` styles if you adopt the callouts. The `.stage` rules in `juice.css` (heat glow, `::after` wash) assume `position:relative` and `overflow:hidden` on `.stage`.
10. Looks: include `themes.css` after `juice.css`, set `data-skin` to the new ids, and add the Look and sound sheet (markup and `optsHtml`/`syncOpts` in the demo). Set `g.menu=true` while the sheet is open and skip time advancement in `tick()` when it is set.

## Tuning knobs

- Particle count and power: `J.correct` (`10+streak*2`, `4.5+streak*.22`).
- Streak where glow maxes out: `Juice.heat` divides by 20.
- Tier thresholds and names: `TIERS` in `juice.js`.
- Curtain length: `switchTo` `ms` and the `.66s` in `jx-curtain`.
- Overall loudness: `master.gain` (0.9) and the per-sound volume arguments.

## Key map and how the new pieces fit it

Game keys (set by the speed-game thread): Tab hint, Enter skip, Ctrl+Enter restart run, Esc quit run. Buttons: Restart run and Quit run under the card, next to Look & sound. Switch's old Space-to-skip is gone.

- **Slides:** Enter (or a tap) ends a slide early. Because Enter also skips a question, the demo ignores Enter for 350 ms after a slide ends and ignores held-down repeats, so one press can never dismiss a slide and skip the next question. No question is on screen during a slide, so nothing else clashes. Esc and Ctrl+Enter still quit and restart during a slide (both dismiss it first).
- **Look & sound sheet:** while it is open the timer is paused and the game keys are ignored. Esc closes the sheet (it does not quit the run), and Enter or Ctrl+Enter do nothing until it is closed. The sheet is opened by button only, so no key is spent on it.
- **Tab:** the hint key. Do not let Tab move focus out of the answer box during a run (the demo calls `preventDefault`); wire it to the real hint.
- **Restart:** `restartRun()` ends the current run (dismissing any slide and the low-time effects) and starts a fresh one in the same mode.
- Update the on-screen hint text and the skip bar label to "Enter".

## Revision 3 (popup cards, time bar, results layout)

- **Streak and double points are a popup card.** `Juice.slide` now hides the puzzle card and shows a same-sized card in its place (type colour border and glow, rotated big title like "Warm" / "On fire" / "Double points", the multiplier chip). It pops in with a bounce, stays about 1.2 s, then pops out and the next question appears. Tap or Enter ends it early. It copies the card's corner shape, so it matches every look.
- **Time bar:** thicker (18 px), drains left as a filled bar with a bright glowing edge, ten-second tick marks, and steps green, amber (last 40%), red (last 10 s, with the pulse). The fill is now driven by `style.width` (not `scaleX`) so the edge stays sharp: `railFill.style.width=(left/RUN*100)+'%'` and `rail.dataset.zone='good'|'mid'|'low'`. If you meant a vertical bar that slides down the side instead, say so and it is a small change.
- **Results screen order:** score, summary line, **Restart run and Back buttons**, level bar, by-type bars, earned, then Your answers last. The buttons are visible without scrolling.

## Revision 4

- **Double points timer:** the strip under the time bar is now a draining bar: a fill that shrinks over the 10 seconds, the label "Double points" and a seconds countdown. Drive it with `evtFill.style.width=(remaining/10*100)+'%'`.
- **Cyberpunk sharpened:** removed the blurry parts (double-offset text shadows on every number, a full-screen scanline layer over the text, inner glow on the card, glow on the pips).
